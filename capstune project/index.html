violet evergarden

Violet Evergarden is a critically acclaimed anime series produced by Kyoto Animation that follows the emotional journey of a young woman raised as a child soldier during a war similar to World War I. After losing both her arms in battle, Violet is fitted with mechanical prosthetics and becomes an Auto Memory Doll, a ghostwriter who helps others express their deepest feelings through letters. Her primary motivation is to understand the final words of her former commander, Major Gilbert Bougainvillea, who told her "I love you" before disappearing in action, a phrase she initially struggles to comprehend.

pirates

Pirates were maritime outlaws who operated primarily during the late 17th to early 18th centuries, known for their daring raids on merchant ships and coastal settlements. While often romanticized as swashbuckling adventurers, most were ordinary people driven by economic necessity or a desire for freedom from societal norms.
